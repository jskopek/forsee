# What the iOS Simulator wire test found

The wire test (`engine/tests/realapp_phone_app.py`, CI job "iOS app in the Simulator talks to
the engine (test pattern)") runs the real Eye Capture app in the iOS Simulator with its synthetic
test-pattern camera and talks to it with the engine's own phone client. It was added in ddfcbe9
and made to pass in aad51e6 and b817b08. These are the real problems between the app and the
engine it found, and what happened to each. None of this has been seen on a real iPhone yet.

| #   | Found                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Side   | Status                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **The engine restarted the phone's camera every 10 s on a cold start.** The first estimator run loads MediaPipe (and on a fresh Mac builds matplotlib's font cache): 28 s in CI. The no-frames watchdog counted _processed_ frames, so while frames were arriving but waiting it logged "No frames from the phone for 3 s / 13 s / 23 s" and reconfigured the camera each time; the stream client was replaced 11 times and 0 frames were processed in 30 s (run 43951077743). | Engine | Fixed: the watchdog now counts frames _received_ (`pipeline.py`, `_last_received_at`). The test also warms the estimators up first. On the owner's first real run, expect the first gaze to take up to about 30 s after connecting; that is the model load, not a broken phone. |
| 2   | **A slow route stalled the stream.** `/api/configure` blocks until the camera runs; it ran on the same queue as all socket I/O, so live-stream writes and other requests waited behind it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | App    | Fixed in ddfcbe9: routes run on their own serial queue.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 3   | **`isListening` and `stop()` touched the network listener off its queue** (a thread-safety bug in the app's HTTP server).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | App    | Fixed in ddfcbe9: state behind a lock.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 4   | **The `dropped` count in frame headers lost frames** dropped while another frame was being encoded, so the engine under-reported phone-side drops.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | App    | Fixed in ddfcbe9.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 5   | **The engine process hung at exit on macOS after using MediaPipe** (15 minutes in CI; "face landmarker unavailable: cannot schedule new futures after interpreter shutdown").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Engine | Fixed: the test closes it (b817b08) and `Engine.stop()` now closes it after the frame worker finishes (183a5a8); CI fails a hang after 10 minutes.                                                                                                                              |

Checked and found to agree (9 tests, passing in CI): status, clock sync and hello; frame
rectangles, scales and coordinates in the PGZ1 header against where the test pattern really drew
its discs; zoom reported and drawn; lens settings round trip; bad requests answered as JSON errors;
status messages and reconnect; stream HTTP headers and back-pressure; the synthetic ARKit block and
ARKit eye crops; the engine processing the app's stream (about 18 fps processed of 23 fps sent on a
CI Mac, the rest dropped as "newest frame wins").

Not covered by the Simulator: the real camera and ARKit, real lens and zoom behaviour, Wi-Fi
between two devices, the Local Network permission prompts on the iPhone and the Mac, heat and
battery.
